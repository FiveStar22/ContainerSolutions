What We Offer

At Container Solutions, we provide a comprehensive range of container-related services tailored to your needs, whether for storage, construction, or custom projects. Our professional team ensures quality and timely delivery of all services.

Core Services

Container Sales

High-quality new and pre-owned containers available for purchase. Custom sizes and modifications are also available to suit your specific requirements.

  • Wide range of sizes (20ft, 40ft, etc.)
  • Custom modifications available
  • Competitive pricing

Container Rentals

Flexible rental options for short-term or long-term projects. We offer containers in excellent condition, ready for immediate use.

  • Short-term & long-term rentals
  • Delivery & pickup included
  • Ideal for construction sites, events, storage

Custom Container Solutions

Design and build custom container units such as offices, homes, pop-up shops, or specialized storage units tailored to your specifications.

  • Interior & exterior customization
  • Electrical & plumbing setups
  • Interior partitioning & branding

Modifications & Upgrades

Transform your container with insulation, ventilation, windows, doors, or branding to meet your unique needs.

  • Insulation & climate control
  • Electrical & plumbing setup
  • Interior design & branding
