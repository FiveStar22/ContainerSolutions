Who We Are

Established in 2022, Container Solutions (PTY) LTD specializes in providing high-quality container solutions for a variety of needs. Whether you're looking to purchase a durable container, build a custom container home, or rent containers for your project, we are your trusted partner.

Our Mission

To deliver innovative, reliable, and affordable container solutions—including sales, rentals, and custom modifications—that support our clients' construction, business, and residential needs.

What We Offer

  • New and Pre-Owned Containers for Sale and Rent
  • Custom Container Homes & Modular Buildings
  • Container Modifications and Conversions
  • Long-term and Short-term Container Rentals
  • Delivery and Logistics Services

Our Commitment

We are committed to quality, safety, and customer satisfaction. Our team works closely with clients to understand their unique needs and deliver tailored container solutions that meet the highest standards.
